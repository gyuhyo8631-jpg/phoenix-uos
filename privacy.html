Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This page is prepared for the current KOREA EASY web service and Android launch preparation. It must be updated whenever production data collection, accounts, payments, analytics, location permissions, or third-party integrations change.

1. Information we may process

The current service may process information you enter to use travel features, such as selected airports and destinations. If account, booking, payment, saved trip, coupon, location, or messaging features are activated later, this policy will be updated before those features collect personal information.

2. Purpose

Information is used to provide travel guidance, improve service reliability, save user-requested trip information, and support features explicitly selected by the user.

3. Third-party services

Some links may open third-party services such as maps, hotels, transport providers, travel experiences, or retailers. Their own privacy policies apply after you leave KOREA EASY.

4. Location

KOREA EASY does not require precise location for the basic airport-to-destination guide. If a future nearby feature requests device location, permission will be requested before access.

5. Data retention and deletion

Where account-based data is introduced, KOREA EASY will provide clear retention rules and a method to request deletion. This policy will be updated with the exact process before launch of that feature.

6. Children

The service is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent required by applicable law.

7. Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Official support contact information will be published here before public Android release.
