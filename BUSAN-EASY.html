WALKING + TRAIN GUIDE

From the arrivals hall to the light rail

1Leave the International or Domestic arrivals hall.
2Follow signs for “Light Rail / 경전철”.
3Cross the terminal frontage and enter Airport Station (공항역).
4Buy or charge a transport card, pass the gate, then check the train direction.
Toward Sasang · 사상 방면Use for Line 2: Seomyeon, Gwangalli and Haeundae. For Nampo, change to Line 1 at Seomyeon.
Toward Daejeo · 대저 방면Use for Line 3 and connections through Daejeo. Check the platform display before boarding.
Airport shops, routes and operating times can change. Confirm current information at the airport.
